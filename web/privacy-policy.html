Privacy Policy

CalcAd is an educational calculator designed to explain mathematical results. This Privacy Policy describes how CalcAd handles information when you use the web or PWA version of the service.

Information We Collect

CalcAd does not collect personal data. It has no user accounts, login system, advertising, or analytics. Calculation inputs and results remain in the browser or on the device and are not remotely transmitted.

CalcAd does not collect location, contacts, camera, microphone, health, biometric, or financial-account data. It does not process payments or subscriptions.

How CalcAd Works

Calculations and explanations are performed locally in your browser or on your device. The application does not send calculation inputs or results to CAUCO or another remote service.

Data Sharing

Because CalcAd does not collect personal data or calculation data, it does not share user data with third parties.

Local Storage and Offline Use

The CalcAd service worker caches local application assets so the app can load and work offline after it has been loaded once. This cache contains application files, not a user account or a remote copy of your calculations.

Children and Educational Use

CalcAd may be used for learning and educational purposes, including by children. Since CalcAd does not collect personal data, it is not designed to request or retain information about children.

Third-Party Services

CalcAd does not use third-party advertising, analytics, account, payment, or calculation services. The application is intended to run locally in the browser or on the device.

Security

CalcAd limits data exposure by performing calculations locally and not transmitting calculation data to a remote service. You should still keep your browser, device, and operating system up to date and use the normal security protections available on your device.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

CAUCO may update this Privacy Policy if CalcAd’s implementation or privacy practices change. The effective date at the top of this page indicates when this version took effect.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact CAUCO at claudio@aisosu.fi.
